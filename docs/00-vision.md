# 00 — Vision & Operating Model

## What NextBridge is
A German-based venture that builds software which runs business processes for the Mittelstand:
Automatisierung, KI-Agenten, Dashboards, Individualsoftware. Positioning: **built in Germany,
GDPR-first, engineering-grade**. The name: the bridge from how a company works today to what
runs by itself tomorrow.

## Roles
- Partner A (German): sales, network, client relationships, German legal/admin coordination.
- Partner B (technical founder): solution design, delivery, quality, this repo.

## Phases
1. **Freelance phase (now):** operate lean, deliver 1–3 pilot projects via Partner A's network,
   build reference cases. No employees, no outsourcing.
2. **Proof phase:** 3+ paying clients, documented cases with numbers, productized packages priced.
3. **Company phase:** register UG/GmbH, revisit subcontracting (e.g., India network) only with
   proper AVV/GDPR structure — currently explicitly out of scope.

## Non-goals (v1)
- Cybersecurity as a service line (we say "secure by design", we don't sell security).
- Outsourced delivery. Our moat is "your data and your developer are in Germany."
- Building a custom multi-agent framework. We configure Claude Code's native machinery.
