# Research: DIN 5008 letterhead and postal direct mail

Date: 2026-10-19. One researcher agent, for the Word letterhead of D-071. The DIN 5008 and
DIN 680 norm texts cost money and were **not read**; every DIN value below comes from secondary
sources. Several primary hosts (gesetze-im-internet.de, eur-lex, datenschutzkonferenz-online.de)
did not resolve from the sandbox, so the UWG, GDPR and DSK texts were read through search
snippets only. This note records requirements and sources. It contains no drafted legal text.

## Question

What does DIN 5008 Form B fix for a German business letter, which envelopes fit it, and what do
§ 7 UWG and the GDPR require of an introduction letter sent by post to prospective clients?

## DIN 5008 Form B

Edition: DIN 5008:2020-03 plus Berichtigung 1:2020-07, current per DIN Media
(dinmedia.de/de/norm/din-5008/318422674). It is a recommendation, not a law.

| Element | Value (mm) | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Letterhead band | 0–45 from the top | Form A is 27 |
| Address field | x 20–105, y 45–90 (85 × 45) | text starts at x 25, aligned with the body |
| Zusatz- und Vermerkzone | 17.7 high, 5 lines, y 45–62.7 | holds the return line; some sources give 12.7 / 3, read here as Form A or older (unverified) |
| Return line (Rücksendeangabe) | one small line in that zone | 6–8 pt per secondary sources |
| Anschriftzone | 27.3 high, max 6 lines, y 62.7–90 | type at least 8 pt; no blank line between street and town |
| Information block | from x 125, y 50; at most 75 wide | one source says x 124 |
| Margins | left 25; right at least 20 | one source says right 15 |
| Betreff | two blank lines below the field, about y 98.5 | no "Betreff" label |
| Spacing | Betreff → salutation 2 blank lines; salutation → body 1; paragraphs 1; body → Grußformel 1 | no source found for the often-quoted three lines before the typed name |
| Fold marks | y 105 and 210 | **derived**: Form A's 87 / 192 plus the 18 mm Form A/B shift in BayAGO Anlage 2 |
| Hole mark | y 148.5 | centre of the sheet |
| Bottom margin, follow pages, page numbers | unresolved | sources disagree (20 or 25; "Seite 2 von 3" or "- 2 -") |

Typography: salutation ends with a comma and the body continues in lowercase; Grußformel without
a comma; date ISO `2026-10-19` preferred, `19.10.2026` accepted, normally the last line of the
information block; phone numbers in the international form `+49 <area code without 0> <number>`,
no slashes, no `(0)`.

## Envelopes

A Form B sheet folded at 105 / 210 mm fits a **DL** window envelope (110 × 220; window 90 × 45 at
20 from the left, 15 from the bottom, per DIN 680 as cited by de.wikipedia "DIN lang") and a
**C6/5** (114 × 229, same window, secondary sources). The fit is the researcher's arithmetic: the
folded top panel puts the field at 50–95 mm in the DL window's 50–95 mm. Test a printed sample in
a real envelope before ordering in bulk.

## § 7 UWG — advertising letters to businesses

A B2B advertising **letter** needs no prior consent: § 7(2) UWG lists phone, automated calls,
fax and e-mail as always unreasonable without consent, not post. § 7(1) still forbids
advertising the addressee recognisably does not want — after an objection, stop. IHK München
and IHK Bodensee-Oberschwaben agree that letter advertising is generally allowed. A channel UWG
forbids leaves no legitimate interest under the GDPR either.

## GDPR — a letter to a named person

- Names and contact details of people acting for a company are personal data (Art. 4(1);
  CJEU C-710/23, 3 April 2025).
- Basis: Art. 6(1)(f) with a documented balancing; Recital 47 allows direct marketing as a
  legitimate interest, case by case (DSK Orientierungshilfe Direktwerbung, 18 Feb 2022).
- **Source of the name:** the authorities regard taking contact data from a website's Impressum
  for advertising as generally unlawful (DSK guidance 2022 p. 15, via secondary sources).
- **Art. 14:** information at the latest with the first communication (Art. 14(3)(b)),
  including the source of the data and the legitimate interest.
- **Art. 21(2)–(4):** the right to object to direct marketing at any time, pointed out
  explicitly and separately from other information, at the latest in the first communication.
- LfDI Baden-Württemberg (competent for Walldorf) recommends keeping objectors on a suppression
  list rather than deleting them. Its position on layered notices was not found.

**Elements a notice has to cover** (elements, not text): controller identity and contact;
purpose and legal basis; the legitimate interest; categories of data; recipients (e.g. a
lettershop); storage period incl. the suppression list; the source of the address; the data
subject rights; the right to complain to a supervisory authority; the right to object to direct
marketing, set apart; how to object.

**Unnamed addressee.** Recital 14 excludes legal persons' data, so a letter to "XY GmbH,
Geschäftsführung" arguably triggers no Art. 14 / 21(4) duty (LfDI BW leaflet, Jan 2020, agrees
in principle for consumers). Not so for sole traders, e.K. and Freiberufler, whose business data
is personal data, and unsettled for one-person GmbHs (OLG Dresden 4 U 1377/22 left it open).
No authority statement on the exact "an die Geschäftsführung" case was found.

## Conflict with the repo, for the founders

D-068 removed the street address from printed collateral and shortened the names. A posted letter
normally carries a postal return address, and Art. 14(1)(a) asks for the controller's identity and
contact details — the same gap D-068 already logged for § 5 DDG and Art. 13. Not resolved here.
