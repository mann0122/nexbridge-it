Stage 01 — scatter

One object, rearranging.

Every particle keeps a stable identity across shapes, so the morph reads as a single object being reorganised rather than two images crossfading. That is the whole trick, and it is the reason this looks engineered instead of decorative.

Stage 02 — bridge

The brand metaphor, built in.

A suspension bridge is one of the library's stock shapes — cables, towers, deck and hangers, authored as line segments and extruded into two planes. It arrives on-brand before anyone customises anything.

Stage 03 — schematic (custom)

Authored in drawing grammar.

Three process blocks, directed connectors with arrowheads, instrument tag bubbles and a feedback return path — a P&ID signal flow written with line(), arc() and rect() in unit space, then extruded by twin().

Stage 04 — dimensioned (custom)

Measured, not styled.

A part outline carrying real dimension lines: extension lines, arrow terminators, bore circles. The field can draw the same vocabulary the rest of the site uses in flat SVG.

Stage 05 — title block (custom)

The Zeichnungskopf, in three dimensions.

The recurring bordered mini-table with drawing-frame corner ticks. Holds at the end of the scroll rather than resolving into nothing.
